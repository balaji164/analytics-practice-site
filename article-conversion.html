Understanding conversion rate

Conversion rate tells you how frequently visitors complete a defined business action.

The basic idea

Conversion rate is generally calculated as conversions divided by the relevant number of users or sessions, depending on the analysis.

Choose meaningful actions

A contact submission, demo request or purchase is usually more useful than treating every click as a conversion.
